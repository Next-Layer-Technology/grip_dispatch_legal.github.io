Verbal SMS Consent Script

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Purpose

Grip Dispatch may contact customers and drivers by SMS for service-related communications, including load updates, driver status updates, delivery notifications, appointment updates, account notifications, and other communications related to services requested through Grip Dispatch.

Verbal Consent Script

Grip Dispatch Representative:

“Before we send you text messages, I need your permission to contact you by SMS.

Grip Dispatch will use text messages to send you [service-related updates such as load status, driver updates, delivery notifications, appointment updates, and other account or service notifications].

Message frequency may vary depending on your activity with Grip Dispatch. Message and data rates may apply.

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. For help, reply HELP or contact Grip Dispatch customer support.

Do you agree to receive these text messages from Grip Dispatch at the mobile number you provided?”

Customer:

“Yes, I agree.”

Grip Dispatch Representative:

“Thank you. Your consent has been recorded. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP.”

If the Customer Declines

Grip Dispatch Representative:

“No problem. We will not enroll you in SMS messaging. You can still continue using Grip Dispatch services without providing SMS consent.”

Consent Record

When verbal consent is provided, the Grip Dispatch representative records:

  • Customer/driver name
  • Mobile phone number
  • Date and time of consent
  • Name/ID of the Grip Dispatch representative who obtained consent
  • Exact purpose of the SMS messages
  • Confirmation that the customer verbally agreed to receive SMS messages
  • Any applicable account or customer reference

These records are retained by Grip Dispatch as evidence of consent.

Opt-Out

Customers can revoke their SMS consent at any time by replying STOP to a Grip Dispatch message.

Customers may also contact Grip Dispatch customer support to request that SMS communications be stopped.

Confirmation Message

Where applicable, after verbal consent is recorded, Grip Dispatch may send a confirmation SMS such as:

“Grip Dispatch: You are now subscribed to receive service-related text messages from Grip Dispatch. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out.”
