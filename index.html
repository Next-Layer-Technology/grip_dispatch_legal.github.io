How we work with you

SMS consent comes first — every time

We do not send service-related text messages until the customer or driver has expressly agreed to receive them. Here is how consent and communication are handled.

1

A service need is identified

A customer requests service, or a driver needs service-related dispatch communication.

2

We ask for permission

Before sending texts, a Grip Dispatch representative explains the SMS program and asks for explicit verbal consent at the mobile number provided.

3

We coordinate the work

Texts are limited to requested-service communications such as load, driver-status, delivery, appointment, account, and other service updates — not marketing.

4

You stay in control

You can revoke consent at any time by replying STOP or by contacting Grip Dispatch customer support.

SMS consent, in plain terms

Text messaging is opt-in and limited to service-related communication — never marketing. Message and data rates may apply, and message frequency varies with your service activity.

Reply STOP at any time to opt out of SMS, or HELP for assistance. You can also contact customer support to ask us to stop.

Full details are in our Verbal SMS Consent Script, SMS Terms & Conditions, and Privacy Policy.
