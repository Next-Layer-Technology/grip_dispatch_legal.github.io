Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Grip Dispatch ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our mobile mechanic dispatch services and communications with customers and drivers.

This Privacy Policy applies to Grip Dispatch and its family of regional service brands, including but not limited to: Chicagoland Auto Response (collectively, the "Grip Dispatch Family of Brands"). Each brand operates under this same policy regardless of the regional service area or brand name displayed to the customer.

1. Information We Collect

When you request, receive, or help provide a Grip Dispatch service, our admin team may create a customer or driver information file that includes:

  • Your name and contact information, including your phone number
  • Vehicle information and service/job details
  • Service location and appointment details
  • Your SMS consent status and the details showing how and when consent was obtained or revoked
  • Communications exchanged with our admin team or mechanics during the course of a job

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Coordinate and dispatch mobile mechanic services you have requested
  • Send customers and drivers requested-service communications after obtaining SMS consent
  • Maintain accurate service and repair records
  • Respond to future service inquiries you initiate using the same contact information
  • Improve our internal dispatch operations

3. How SMS Communication Works

We do not send service-related SMS messages until the customer or driver has expressly agreed to receive them. Specifically:

  • A Grip Dispatch representative reads our verbal SMS consent disclosure before enrollment.
  • The disclosure explains the service-related message types, variable frequency, possible message and data rates, and the STOP and HELP instructions.
  • The customer or driver must expressly agree to receive SMS at the mobile number provided.
  • We record the consent details as evidence of that agreement.
  • We do not use this consent to send marketing or promotional messages.

4. Data Retention

We retain customer and driver information, including consent, opt-out, job, and communication records, for our business, compliance, and record-keeping purposes. Retained information is not used for marketing or promotional SMS.

5. Opting Out of SMS

You may decline or revoke SMS consent at any time by replying STOP to a Grip Dispatch message or by contacting customer support and asking us to stop SMS communications.

6. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Mechanics/contractors assigned to fulfill your specific service request
  • Service providers who support our dispatch platform, payment processing, or SMS/communications infrastructure (e.g., Twilio), solely to enable the services described in this policy
  • Law enforcement or regulators when required by law

Mobile information, including phone numbers and SMS consent records, is not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

7. Your Communication Controls

You can review our SMS Terms & Conditions and Verbal SMS Consent Script on this website. Reply STOP to revoke SMS consent, reply HELP for assistance, or contact customer support directly.
