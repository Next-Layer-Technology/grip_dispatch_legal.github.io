Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Grip Dispatch ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our mobile mechanic dispatch services, admin platform, and client and mechanic mobile applications.

This Privacy Policy applies to Grip Dispatch and its family of regional service brands, including but not limited to: Chicagoland Auto Response (collectively, the "Grip Dispatch Family of Brands"). Each brand operates under this same policy regardless of the regional service area or brand name displayed to the customer.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us to request service, our admin team creates a customer information file that may include:

  • Your name and contact information, including your phone number
  • Vehicle information and service/job details
  • Service location and appointment details
  • Your stated preference for how we contact you regarding a specific job (phone call or SMS text message)
  • Communications exchanged with our admin team or mechanics during the course of a job

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Coordinate and dispatch mobile mechanic services you have requested
  • Communicate with you about the specific job or contract you initiated, using the contact method (phone or SMS) you selected for that request
  • Maintain accurate service and repair records
  • Respond to future service inquiries you initiate using the same contact information
  • Improve our internal dispatch operations

3. How SMS Communication Works

All text message communication with our company is customer-initiated. We do not send unsolicited text messages. Specifically:

  • You contact us first, by phone or SMS, to request service.
  • Our admin team asks whether you would like communication for that specific job to occur via phone call or SMS text message.
  • If you select SMS, that authorization applies to the back-and-forth communication needed to coordinate and complete that particular job only.
  • Once a job is complete, we do not proactively text you to solicit future work. If you wish to arrange future service, you may do so through our mobile app or by contacting us again, which we treat as your authorization to respond to that new, customer-initiated inquiry.

4. Data Retention

We retain customer information, including past job and communication records, for our business and record-keeping purposes. Retained information is never used to proactively contact you about new or future services. Any future contact from us will only occur in direct response to an inquiry you initiate.

5. Opting Out of SMS

You may decline SMS communication at any time. You can disable SMS communications directly within the mobile app account settings, or by replying STOP to any text message thread. You may also request account and communication support through the mobile app or by contacting us directly.

6. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Mechanics/contractors assigned to fulfill your specific service request
  • Service providers who support our dispatch platform, payment processing, or SMS/communications infrastructure (e.g., Twilio), solely to enable the services described in this policy
  • Law enforcement or regulators when required by law

7. Your Account Controls

Through our mobile app, you can view your account information, review Terms & Conditions, manage SMS communication preferences, and contact support.
