Last updated: September 18, 2026

These Terms apply to SMS communications sent by Grip Dispatch and its family of regional service brands, including Chicagoland Auto Response.

1. Program Description

Grip Dispatch operates a mobile mechanic dispatch service. We use SMS to communicate with customers and drivers about services requested through Grip Dispatch, including load updates, driver status updates, delivery notifications, appointment updates, account notifications, and other service-related communications.

Before sending these messages, a Grip Dispatch representative asks for the customer’s or driver’s express verbal consent at the mobile number provided. This program does not send marketing or promotional messages.

2. How Consent Is Obtained

Consent to receive SMS messages is obtained verbally before enrollment. The representative explains the types of service-related messages, that message frequency varies, that message and data rates may apply, and how to use STOP or HELP. The customer or driver must affirmatively agree to receive the messages.

Grip Dispatch records the consenting person’s name and mobile number, the date and time, the representative’s name or ID, the exact SMS purpose, the verbal agreement, and any applicable account or customer reference. Phone numbers are provided in connection with Grip Dispatch services and are not obtained from purchased lists.

3. Message Frequency

Message frequency varies depending on the recipient’s activity with Grip Dispatch. Messages are limited to service-related communications.

5. Opt-Out Instructions

You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP to any message or by contacting Grip Dispatch customer support. After opting out, you will not receive further SMS messages unless you provide consent again.
